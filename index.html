About

I am a second-year PhD student at MIT in the Disruptive Planet group. In my research I use a variety of flavours of the transit method to study the characteristics of exoplanets and their hosts stars.

I am particularly interested in figuring out how the heterogeneous surface of stars impacts the measurements we make of exoplanet properties. You can read more about my research here and check out my full list of publications here.

Outside of academia, I really enjoy rock climbing, running, and making some delicious food!
